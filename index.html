(01) Featured product Healthcare · 2026

Medical CRM

Occupational health exams, finally digital.

Every year, every employee in hazardous work in Kazakhstan has to pass a medical exam with up to seven specialists. Most clinics still run that on paper and a 1C database from another decade. Medical CRM replaces it: one platform for doctors, clinic admins and employers — from the first appointment to the signed fitness-for-work conclusion.

Scope
Product design, web app, mobile capture, reporting
Users
Doctors · clinic admins · employers
Compliance
Built around order ҚР ДСМ-131/2020
medical-crm / exams Production build
01

Phone in, paper out

No scanner. No USB sticks. Point a phone at the screen.

ECGs, X‑rays and lab results arrive on paper. In Medical CRM the doctor opens the patient’s files, a QR code appears, the nurse photographs the pages — and they land in the record on the desktop seconds later, tagged with type and date.

  • Multi‑page capture with automatic compression
  • Every file linked to the exam, the specialist and the year
  • Full audit trail: who opened, signed and closed what, and when

 Questions we hear a lot 
 
 How quickly can you start? 
Usually within one to two weeks of an agreed scope. The discovery phase itself can start the same week we talk.
 
 
 Do you work with clients outside Kazakhstan? 
Yes. We work remotely with teams anywhere. UTC+5 gives a comfortable overlap with European mornings and Asian afternoons, and all communication can run in English.
 
 
 How do you price a project? 
After the discovery call you get a fixed price for the first milestone — no open‑ended hourly billing. Larger products are split into milestones, each with its own scope and price. Ongoing support runs on a simple monthly retainer.
 
 
 Who owns the code? 
You do — fully. Repositories, design files, cloud accounts and credentials are yours from day one.
 
 
 Can you take over an existing project? 
Yes. We start with a short code and infrastructure audit, tell you honestly what we found, and propose a plan: fix, refactor or rebuild only what really needs it.
